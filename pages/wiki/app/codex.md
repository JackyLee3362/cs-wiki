## 识别 skills 的目录

1. 系统内置 skill
   - ~/.codex/skills/.system/

2. 本地 agents skill
   - ~/.agents/skills/

3. 插件缓存里的 bundled skills
   - ~/.codex/plugins/cache/openai-bundled/

4. 插件缓存里的 primary-runtime skills
   - ~/plugins/cache/openai-primary-runtime/
